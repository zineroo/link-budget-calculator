Thresholds are the ideal (quasi-error-free, AWGN, normal FECFRAME, no pilots) Es/N₀ values from ETSI EN 302 307. Real modems need roughly 0.5–1 dB more. This models the uplink only; a full end-to-end budget would also combine the downlink and transponder intermodulation C/N.
